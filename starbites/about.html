About Star Bites

Who We Are

Star Bites is a small family-friendly dessert business in Soshanguve that was established in early 2026. It specialises in providing delicious and high-quality treats to customers.

We aim to create a memorable experience for every customer by offering a variety of treats made with love and care. Our business provides a casual, welcoming and enjoyable environment for everyone.

Our Story

Star Bites was created to bring enjoyable dessert experiences to our community. We believe that simple treats can create special memories.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide delicious, affordable and quality desserts while giving customers friendly service and a welcoming experience.

Our Values

We value quality, customer satisfaction, friendliness, creativity and bringing people together through good food.
